Help & Support

For help, questions and bug reports, join us on Discord:

Discord: help, support and bug reports

When reporting a problem, please include:

  • your Blender version and your BagaPie version,
  • what you selected and which tool you used,
  • a screenshot of the BagaPie panel or of the error.

Check Troubleshooting first: it covers the most common issues.

You can also contact us through SuperHive.
