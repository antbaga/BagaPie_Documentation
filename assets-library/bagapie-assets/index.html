BagaPie Assets

BagaPie is free and open source. Its development and maintenance are funded by BagaPie Assets, a library of ready-to-scatter assets made for BagaPie. Buying it is the most direct way to support the add-on.

Current prices and editions are on the product page.

Made for BagaPie

  • Scatter in one click. Select assets in the Asset Browser and use Scatter Selection, or any tool with an Asset Browser button.
  • Scatter presets. The Full edition includes 40 presets for the GeoPack pie menu (V): pick a surface and apply a complete environment. See Scatter presets.
  • Smart shaders. Grass, plant, tree, rock and stump materials can be tweaked from the BagaPie panel: tint, brightness, saturation with random variations, ambient occlusion, and a Season slider for vegetation. See Material panels.
  • Textures: procedural, 2K, or up to 4K UDIM depending on the asset.
  • Automatic setup. Packs install from the BagaPie preferences and appear as a library in the Asset Browser. See Setup & installation.

Compatibility

BagaPie Assets packs work with BagaPie 9 and newer, in Cycles and Eevee.

License

Assets (models, textures and node trees) are royalty-free for personal and commercial work. They cannot be resold or shared, even modified. See the product page for the full terms.

Also available

  • Baga Pack, a bundle with Baga Ivy Generator, BagaPie Assets Full and Baga Rain Generator. See More add-ons.
  • A Tips option on the product page, if you want to support BagaPie without buying assets.
