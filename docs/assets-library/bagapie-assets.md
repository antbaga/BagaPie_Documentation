## Made for BagaPie

- **Scatter in one click.** Select assets in the Asset Browser and use [Scatter Selection](../object-mode/scatter.md#asset-browser-panel), or any tool with an **Asset Browser** button.
- **Scatter presets.** The Full edition includes 40 presets for the GeoPack pie menu (**V**): pick a surface and apply a complete environment. See [Scatter presets](../geopack/scatter-presets.md).
- **Smart shaders.** Grass, plant, tree, rock and stump materials can be tweaked from the BagaPie panel: tint, brightness, saturation with random variations, ambient occlusion, and a **Season** slider for vegetation. See [Material panels](libraries.md#material-panels).
- **Textures:** procedural, 2K, or up to 4K UDIM depending on the asset.
- **Automatic setup.** Packs install from the BagaPie preferences and appear as a library in the Asset Browser. See [Setup & installation](libraries.md#install-the-packs).

## Compatibility

BagaPie Assets packs work with BagaPie 9 and newer, in Cycles and Eevee.
